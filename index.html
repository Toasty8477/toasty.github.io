ESPHome Plant Monitor

An ESPHome based plant monitor. Monitors soil moisture with a capacitive soil sensor for each plant and monitors ambient light, temperature, and humidity on the main device. All soil sensors are routed back to one central device to save on cost. The device can also talk with Home Assistant to send you notifications when your plant is low on water.

Image Manipulator 3000

Another school project. This is a program that can open images, apply different filters to them, and save them. It supports opening and saving .jpeg and .png files as well as two custom formats .msoe, a plaintext format, and .bmsoe, a binary format. The program uses a two window JavaFX GUI for user interaction.
